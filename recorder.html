IMU Recorder

Connect a WitMotion sensor over Bluetooth, watch X, Y, Z and resultant acceleration and the sensor's orientation live, record a trial and save it as a file for the Motion Data Analyzer.

1. Connect

Hit the ‘Connect’ button to open a window that will show a list of nearby WT IMUs available by Bluetooth. Make sure you choose the one with your IMU’s number (on the back of the sensor). When you finish press ‘Disconnect’ so the IMU is free for another connection (they can only connect to a single device). If you do not see your WT IMU see troubleshooting in the lab.
